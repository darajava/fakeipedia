# Leaked Messages

## World brief

You are building a world where everyone's messages and emails and photos have all been leaked in a huge event.

## Featured article and scope

The Great Leak of 2027 is the main entry point into its own wiki. Treat the target as a flagship featured article of approximately **2,500–3,500 words**, with a substantial introduction, **8–10 developed sections**, useful subsections and a concluding See also section.

All messages are searchable by anyone and by identity
The perpetrators are not known but blackmail authoroties so that the leak is kept online
Identify all aspects of the chaos that ensues.
